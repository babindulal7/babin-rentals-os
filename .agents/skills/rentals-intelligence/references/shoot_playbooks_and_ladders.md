## 2. Shoot-Type Production Playbooks

### 1. Wedding & Event Production
- **Primary Need:** Speed, autofocus accuracy, low-light performance, long continuous battery runtime.
- **Recommended Package:** Sony FX3A + Sony 24-70 GM II + Sony 70-200 GM II + DJI RS4 Pro + DJI Mic 2 Dual.
- **Prohibited / Avoid:** Bulky manual cine primes, anamorphic setups, or unassisted heavy rigs.

### 2. Music Video & Stylized Commercial
- **Primary Need:** Optical character, dynamic range, anamorphic flare, expressive movement.
- **Recommended Package:** RED Komodo-X / DJI Ronin 4D + Blazar Mantis 1.8x Anamorphic / NiSi Athena + Tilta Float + Aputure 600x Pro.
- **Prohibited / Avoid:** Unstabilized sterile consumer lenses without character.

### 3. Documentary & Remote Expedition
- **Primary Need:** Run-and-gun agility, rugged weather protection, battery reserve, dual media redundancy.
- **Recommended Package:** Sony FX3A + Sony 24-105 F4 G + Sigma 24-70 + Dana Dolly + Dual CFexpress A + Card Reader.
- **Mandatory:** Card reader, backup media, and rain protection.

### 4. Brand Commercial & Narrative Film
- **Primary Need:** Repeatable focus marks, zero rolling shutter, client/director wireless feeds, calibrated studio lighting.
- **Recommended Package:** RED Komodo-X + NiSi Athena Prime Set + Nucleus-M + Desview 7-inch OLED + Aputure 600x/300c Grid.
- **Mandatory:** 15mm baseplate rod support, follow focus, and dedicated director monitor.

## 3. Consultative Upsell Rules & Idle Stock Activation

1. **Strategic Benefit Pitching:** Every upsell proposal must cite a concrete production outcome (e.g. *"FX3A gives active cooling and dual native ISO 12,800 for unlit evening wedding receptions"*).
2. **Maximum 3 Recommendations:** Never overwhelm the client with endless options; cap recommendations at 3 prioritized items.
3. **Camera Caretaker Auto-Pitch:** Auto-suggest the Camera Caretaker service (Rs 2,000/day) whenever:
   - Kit rental total is $\ge \text{Rs } 50,000$, or
   - Kit includes RED Komodo, DJI Ronin 4D, CHIOPT, or Blazar Anamorphic lenses.
   - *Pitch:* Protects the client's deposit, provides technical setup assistance, and prevents accidental handling damage.
4. **Idle Capital Activation:** Push under-utilized high-value inventory (e.g. CHIOPT cine zooms, Blazar anamorphic primes) as aspirational tier upgrades when clients request mid-tier packages.

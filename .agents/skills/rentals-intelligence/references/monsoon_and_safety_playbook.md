# Babin Rentals — Monsoon & Environmental Safety Playbook

This reference provides the operating policies for weather extremes, high-altitude shooting, electrical loads, and gimbal payload safety in Nepal.

---

## 1. Monsoon Operating Protocol (June 1 – September 30)

During the Nepal monsoon season (Ashadh to Ashwin), relative humidity in the Kathmandu Valley regularly exceeds 85%, accelerating optical fungus development and electronic corrosion.

### Dispatch Requirements
- **Waterproof Pelican Transport:** All camera bodies and cinema glass must travel in airtight, O-ring-sealed hard cases with fresh silica gel packs.
- **Rain Protection Pack:** Auto-suggested for all outdoor kits (Rs 500/day). Includes body rain cover, front element neoprene cover, and dry microfiber towels.
- **Crew Rain Briefing:** Mandatory verbal reminder to client: *"Never swap lenses in the open rain or in unconditioned humid vehicles."*

### Inbound Return Protocols
- **Immediate Moisture & Fungus Audit:** Every returned lens element is inspected under a high-intensity LED torch for moisture condensation or fungal spores.
- **Dry-Cabinet Quarantine:** Any damp or wet gear is immediately quarantined and placed in an electronic dry cabinet (calibrated to 40%–45% RH) for at least 24 hours before returning to `AVAILABLE`.
- **Wet Decontamination Fee:** Gear returned waterlogged or soaked incurs a standard Rs 5,000 decontamination and ultrasonic drying fee.

### Client Booking Protection
- **Flexible 24-Hour Reschedule:** Outdoor bookings during active monsoon are granted one penalty-free 24-hour weather postponement if rain prevents shooting.

## 2. High-Altitude & Cold-Weather Protocols (Himalayan Shoots)

For shoots in Mustang, Manang, Khumbu, or high-altitude passes ($>3,000\text{ m}$):
- **Battery Capacity Derating:** Cold ambient temperatures ($-10^\circ\text{C}$ to $0^\circ\text{C}$) reduce lithium battery runtime by **35% to 40%**. Rigs require 1.5x to 2x battery reserves.
- **Transport Acclimatization:** When bringing cold gear into warm tea houses or indoor spaces, gear must remain zipped inside cases for 45 minutes to prevent internal moisture condensation.
- **High-Altitude Power:** Inverters and charging stations must be budgeted for generator or solar-powered base camps.

## 3. Electrical & Power Load Safety Limits

- **Standard Circuit Threshold:** In Nepal, standard commercial wall outlets are rated at **230V / 15A** (nominal max continuous load: **2,760 Watts**, allowing a 20% safety margin).
- **Overload Block:** Any combined lighting package exceeding 2,760W on a single circuit (e.g. $4\times \text{Aputure 600x}$) triggers a blocking warning requiring a power distribution board or generator.
- **V-Mount Watt-Hour (Wh) Calculations:**
  $$\text{Runtime (hours)} = \frac{\text{Battery Wh} \times 0.85}{\text{Rig Total Watts}}$$
  *(Where 0.85 represents typical DC-DC conversion efficiency).*

---

## 4. Gimbal & Rig Payload Safety Margin

- **30% Safety Rule:** Motorized gimbals and fluid heads must maintain at least a **30% payload safety margin** below maximum factory rating to prevent motor overheating or hydraulic drift.
  $$\text{Rig Weight} \le \text{Payload Rating} \times 0.70$$
- **Heavy Cinema Zoom Rule:** Any zoom weighing $\ge 2.0\text{ kg}$ (e.g. CHIOPT 28-85mm) requires 15mm baseplate rod support brackets. Operating unsupported on a lightweight gimbal is a hard blocker.

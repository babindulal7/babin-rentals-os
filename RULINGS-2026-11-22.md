# Rulings — 22 November 2026

**Scope:** owner decisions given in chat on 22 Nov 2026 for the first-launch plan. Each ruling records the owner's words, the reading applied, and what it changes. No status on `main` is changed by this file, and nothing is applied to any database.

## R-22.1 — Manager-level approvals can route to the Owner

- **Owner's words:** "We can select name on setting manager to owner."
- **Reading:** Settings gets a choice of who decides Manager-level approvals. The owner can route them to Owner by selecting a name.
- **Changes:** a new setting, not yet built. It only narrows who may approve, so it is stricter (B-1). The default is not set; the owner chooses it in Settings. Affected: damage approvals below Rs 25,000 (Manager), late-fee waivers, extension clashes (T3-29, "the Manager decides"), and the approvals inbox (T5-10). Each module is checked before the build.
- **Revert:** turn the setting off; approvals return to the threshold rule.

## R-22.2 — Online payments are held at launch; payments are entered manually

- **Owner's words:** "hold fone pay and online payment system in launch and do manual entry".
- **Reading:** Fonepay, eSewa and Khalti, online payment links, refunds and the online pay-balance step are not used at launch. Staff record every payment manually.
- **Changes:** T7-05, T7-06, T7-07 and T7-15 are held for launch. The online pay-balance part of T7-08 is held. Statuses are unchanged; they stay `acceptance_pending`. No code changes are made by this ruling.
- **Revert:** enable each payment card once its merchant account is open and the owner approves it.

## R-22.3 — Money is shown in whole rupees, with no paisa

- **Owner's words:** "no paisaa, rs that how it works".
- **Reading:** the existing product rule (`src/lib/money.ts` `formatMoney`, §2.3) shows whole rupees without decimals. A Rs 2,100 request must read Rs 2,100 on the website.
- **Changes:** migration 0263 (`fix/submit-request-paisa`, commit `9b2c6e46`) is approved for review. It is not applied to any database. It is applied to staging only after the 22 Nov staging report's paisa figures are explained, and only with separate owner authorisation.
- **Revert:** re-apply the 0257 definition. Not recommended: request totals would show Rs 21 for a Rs 2,100 request.

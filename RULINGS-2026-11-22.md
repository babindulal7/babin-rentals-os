## R-22.1 — Manager-level approvals can route to the Owner

- **Owner's words:** "We can select name on setting manager to owner."
- **Reading:** Settings gets a choice of who decides Manager-level approvals. The owner can route them to Owner by selecting a name.
- **Changes:** a new setting, not yet built. It only narrows who may approve, so it is stricter (B-1). The default is not set; the owner chooses it in Settings. Affected: damage approvals below Rs 25,000 (Manager), late-fee waivers, extension clashes (T3-29, "the Manager decides"), and the approvals inbox (T5-10). Each module is checked before the build.
- **Revert:** turn the setting off; approvals return to the threshold rule.

# Proposal: Sticker Station 2.0 and Universal QR Identity
Date: 2026-11-26 | Status: owner-approved design direction; implementation pending review
## Non-negotiable operating model
50/50 QR/manual parity means equal functional capability, not an enforced usage ratio. No mandatory scanning or extra approvals merely for manual entry. Every scan action has search, human-readable ID, selection or manual confirmation equivalent. Both converge on existing domain services, authorization, availability checks, lifecycle gates and audit records. Never weaken double-booking, financial integrity or privacy. Scanner hardware is optional for December trial.
## Scope
- One canonical identity per existing equipment unit, customer, order, kit, case, box, warehouse, zone, rack, shelf, compartment, staff record, maintenance job and supported document. Reuse existing database IDs; readable references are separate from opaque, nonsecret QR tokens. No embedded PII, prices, PINs or credentials. Unique indexed mapping and safe idempotent backfill; never recreate records. Unassigned preprinted tokens may be claimed once, transactionally. Retire/revoke tokens, audit changes; reprint retains identity. One canonical active order QR; exceptional security replacement revokes previous token.
- Every order gets a QR upon creation, unchanged across ordinary edits and status transitions. Confirmation email via approved Resend integration includes official logo, QR image, readable order reference and authenticated View My Booking link; customer may present email, screenshot, print or reference. Same canonical order QR on quotes, agreements, invoices, packing/handover/return receipts, reminders, portal and package labels. Package identifiers remain separate and optional, never alternate order identities. Confirmation transition enqueues idempotently; retry without duplicates, show delivery status, allow authorized manual resend with audit. No outbound mail in staging; synthetic preview. Preserve existing paper invoice policy.
- Authenticated staff scan/search resolves exact order and opens appropriate current-stage action: draft/pending, confirmed/prep, packing, pickup, active/overdue, partial return, inspection, cleaning/reset, completed/cancelled. No state mutation from scan alone. Customer portal shows authorized own-order milestones only, never staff notes or restricted financial data. QR is not authentication or pickup authorization. Unknown/retired/wrong-type codes and unauthorized lookups fail safely; rate limit resolver. Optional public found-equipment page minimal company contact only, separate approved scope; no enumeration, internal info or public order details.
- Add Equipment: save and print, save only, bulk units with unique codes, claim preprinted label, existing-unit relabel. Failed printing must not roll back successful registration or duplicate units. Contextual QR/manual parity for customer/order lookup, packing, pickup, return, inspection, maintenance, storage, audit, kit/case and sticker reprint. Scan equipment+location/order/customer/case/area proposes action; authorized confirmation and existing validation required. Batch selection and mixed scan/manual actions share dedupe and checklist state.
- Warehouse hierarchy: warehouse > zone > rack > shelf > compartment; movable box/case/kit has independent stable ID and assigned location. QR and readable labels for receiving, available stock, packing, ready pickup, return, inspection, cleaning/reset, maintenance, quarantine, sub-rental and configurable areas. Location is distinct from lifecycle/availability. Printed contents card is dated snapshot, live contents permission-gated. Print entire rack hierarchy or kit. Category colors distinct from operational status; text always present; yellow attention/in-progress per owner direction.
## Sticker Studio
Tools > Sticker Station: Quick Print, Batch/Global Basket, Equipment, Rack/Shelf, Box/Case, Cable/Accessory, Designer, A4 Smart Layout, Thermal, Templates, Media Stock, Queue/History, Settings; group navigation to avoid clutter. QR Registry accessible from Tools and related records. Add print actions in Inventory, Customers, Orders, Operations, Warehouse, documents. Desktop full workspace; iPad split view; phone compact search/select/preview, sticky action, no mandatory scanner. 44px touch targets, keyboard shortcuts, responsive tables, clear errors, no hidden manual alternatives.
Brand: official logo image (SVG/transparent PNG, monochrome rendition) instead of typed header; small legible handwritten “Property of Babin Rentals” for property labels; bold human-readable ID has priority over QR or decorative text. Five families: Cinema Signature, Industrial Architecture, Tactical Container, Stealth Minimal, Gold Collector. Rack/zone headings, shelf/bin/box, case, dated contents checklists, cable rat-tail/fold-over, QC/maintenance, customer cards, order labels, optional lost-property and tamper-evident templates. Colors only with compatible media/printer; avoid needless solid A4 ink.
Actual ordered black PP rolls (from owner screenshots, reconcile physically): 20x15 (thumbnail conflicts with 25x15), 35x15, 40x20, 40x30, 60x20, 90x40, 100x50 mm. ER-L41 printer profile pending verification; A4 210x297. Additional media: 50mm circular, gold holographic 60x40, colored labels, rat-tail approx 88.9x11.1; verify all geometry. Proposed QR footprints including quiet zone: 10,12,15,20,16,30,35mm respectively; 15mm-high labels default readable ID only until physical test. 40x30 default equipment; 60x20 narrow; 90x40/100x50 cases; 150x50 rack and 200x100 area on A4; customer card 85.6x54. Render real QR; four-module quiet zone, integer printer dots/module, target >=4 dots/module, validate payload version/DPI, contrast, safe margins. Prefer dark modules on opaque white patch over inverse QR on black PP; verify compatible white ribbon and hardware. Never claim physical readiness from preview.
Preserve prior master specification: 54 rectangular, 15 circular, 12 paper/custom and 16 thermal width/custom presets, custom ovals/rotation/favorites. A4 full-sheet mixed-size nesting with exact mm, no scaling, 0/90 rotation, margins/gaps, cutting guides, partial sheet remaining areas; pre-cut sheets/rolls fixed geometry, skipped cells, multi-column pitch/sensor, no arbitrary nesting. Optimize required labels only; suggest optional fillers explicitly. Show sheet/roll usage, waste, pages, printer/media compatibility; actual-size PDF and calibration test. Thermal driver printing first; optional local direct bridge later. Device-specific DPI/printable width/offsets, media type, ribbon, sensor, speed/darkness; never assume ER-L41 capabilities. Versioned templates and profiles; approved field bindings only, no arbitrary code/device commands. Shared design with A4/thermal variants.
Media stock: rolls/sheets/ribbon, installed media, stock and cost estimates, waste, low stock, verified consumption; avoid deduction on ambiguous browser dialog. Queue idempotency, concurrent claims, cancellation, failed/unknown delivery and deliberate retry/reprint. Audit staff, identity, template version, profile, copies, result, reason; printer never changes rental state. RBAC/RLS server-side; owner config, authorized staff use approved templates; public portal cannot access internal print jobs.
Rental Intelligence optional read-only recommendations: label sizes, missing labels, print basket drafts, efficient nesting/media usage. No autonomous printing, record writes, external mail or permission changes during trial.

## Acceptance
- Test unique/concurrent creation, migration/backfill, stable order QR across edits, revocation, duplicate preprinted claim, no duplicate assets/orders, cross-client/staff permission denial and direct API abuse; existing RLS and financial gates unchanged.
- Every core workflow completed without scanner and with mixed manual/QR, same outcome/audit; unknown, damaged, disconnected, duplicate scans, permissions, keyboard, mobile/iPad/desktop, partial pickups/returns, cancelled/completed orders and offline/error states. No false completion.
- Confirmation template includes valid QR, reference, authenticated link; synthetic mail only; idempotent retries, authorized resend, customer isolation; no unintended messages.
- Verify real QR decoding, exact PDF boxes, 27-label A4 portrait and 25-label landscape reference cases from master spec, mixed nesting no overlap, skipped cells, bounds, count, 100% scale, roll geometry and physical measured/scanned tests on identified hardware. Hardware missing = physical verification blocked, not pass.
- Existing repository-prescribed typecheck/lint/unit/RLS/lifecycle/finance/Playwright gates; synthetic staging only. Windows implementation/verification, Mac CI-only per owner constraints. No production changes, deployment, auto-merge or outbound messages. Document evidence and unresolved blockers.

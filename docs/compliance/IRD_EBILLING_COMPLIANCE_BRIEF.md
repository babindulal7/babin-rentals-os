# Nepal IRD Billing Compliance Decision Checklist (T3-05)

**Card:** T3-05 · Paper invoice, VAT register and electronic billing assessment  
**Status:** DRAFT — accountant/IRD interpretation required; not a compliance ruling or production authorization  
**Owner decision:** Babin Rentals currently issues invoices on existing pre-printed paper stock; the OS is not authorized to replace official paper VAT invoices or claim IRD-certified e-billing capability.  
**Reviewed:** 20 November 2026

## Scope
This checklist separates (a) the company's current **pre-printed, sequentially numbered paper VAT invoices** from (b) internal software quotes, receipts, ledgers and record reconciliation. A printed OS-generated PDF is **not automatically an approved IRD tax invoice**. No new billing integration or live setting is authorized by this document.

## Questions for the external accountant / IRD
1. Confirm precisely which legal provisions, notifications, transaction types, business categories and effective dates apply to Babin Rentals at its actual turnover; attach official sources. Do **not** assume an unverified NPR 50 million threshold is a legal exemption.
2. Confirm required paper invoice numbering, fiscal-year reset, retention, duplicate copies, void/cancellation and sequential register rules. Capture the actual pre-printed series already in use.
3. Clarify how the OS should record paper-invoice references, non-VAT transaction records, credit/debit notes, discounts, deposits and partial payments without issuing another official invoice.
4. Confirm timing of VAT recognition on rental service delivery, invoice issuance, advances and cancellation fees; do not hard-code this without accountant sign-off.
5. If and only if mandatory electronic billing is applicable, obtain official IRD guidance on software approval, CBMS reporting, offline operations, corrections and lawful migration from paper stock.

## Draft OS behavior (not active settings)
- Allow an internal booking, estimate, statement or payment receipt that is clearly **not an official tax invoice**.
- Staff record the exact pre-printed paper invoice number and issue date with duplicate prevention and an audit trail.
- An issued paper invoice must not be silently overwritten by OS corrections; accountant-approved accounting adjustments use linked entries with original references.
- Track security deposits as a separate liability, not automatically rental income/VAT.
- Keep tax-sensitive toggles disabled pending written accountant review and a separate owner release approval.

## Evidence required before closing T3-05
- Signed accountant opinion with source links and effective dates.
- Approved sample of current pre-printed paper VAT invoice and numbering register.
- Reconciliation example (paper invoice ↔ booking ↔ payment ↔ VAT ledger).
- Written owner approval for any future live billing process change.

**No automatic tax filing. No claim of IRD-certified e-billing. No live settings changed.**

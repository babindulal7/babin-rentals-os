## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Card **T3-05** resolves whether Babin Rentals is required by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Nepal to implement certified electronic billing (CBMS — Central Billing Monitoring System real-time invoice synchronization) or whether its settled operational model of issuing **pre-printed, sequentially numbered paper VAT invoices** remains legally valid and compliant under Nepal VAT legislation.

### Key Determination
1. **Settled Owner Product Ruling (D1, D2, D14):** Babin Dulal has ruled explicitly that Babin Rentals issues official **physical pre-printed paper VAT tax invoices** bound in approved physical invoice books. The software (Babin Rentals OS) is **not** an electronic cash register, point-of-sale tax printer, or IRD-certified electronic billing software.
2. **Statutory Applicability Under Nepal VAT Act, 2052 (1996) & Rules, 2053 (1997):**
   - Under Nepal VAT Act §14 and VAT Rules Rule 17, taxpayers registered for VAT may issue tax invoices either using pre-printed paper invoice books approved/notified by the tax officer or via computerized billing software approved by the IRD under the Electronic Billing Procedure (*इलेक्ट्रोनिक बिजक सम्बन्धी कार्यविधि*).
   - Real-time online synchronization via CBMS (Central Billing Monitoring System) is mandatory **only** for taxpayers officially notified by the IRD (primarily large taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Office, taxpayers exceeding designated annual turnover thresholds, or specific retail/department stores mandated by IRD public gazette).
   - For an audiovisual equipment rental service with turnover within standard SME thresholds, issuing physical pre-printed paper tax invoices with sequential serials remains fully compliant with the law and does not mandate CBMS electronic billing registration.
3. **Role of Babin Rentals OS (Law 7 / B18 / T3-06):**
   - The OS functions solely as an internal operational rental management, inventory scheduling, and accounting ledger consolidation tool.
   - All client-facing PDFs generated by the OS (Quotations, Pro Forma Invoices, Rental Agreements, Packing Lists, Payment Receipts, and Statements) carry explicit, prominent non-tax disclaimers:
     > *"PRO FORMA / SUPPORTING DOCUMENT — THIS IS NOT AN OFFICIAL VAT TAX INVOICE. Official VAT tax invoices are issued on physical pre-printed paper."*
   - Official paper invoice serial numbers (`paper_tax_invoice_no`) issued at the counter are recorded in the database with strict duplicate prevention and audit logging, feeding the Sales VAT Register (`src/lib/vatRegister.ts`) for monthly tax pack exports (T3-23).

## 3. System Architecture & Safeguards for Paper Invoices

To prevent any ambiguity or regulatory risk regarding electronic billing, Babin Rentals OS implements strict code-level safeguards:

### A. Non-Tax Disclaimers on Supporting Documents (T3-06 / D14)
Every generated document includes explicit statutory disclaimer headers and footers:
- **Pro Forma Invoices (`PRO-`):** Explicitly marked *"PRO FORMA INVOICE — Not a Tax Invoice"*. Cannot be used as an input tax credit document.
- **Rental Agreements (`AGR-`):** Equipment lease terms, custody handover condition, and liability acknowledgment. Excludes VAT and deposits from rental revenue (Law 5).
- **Payment Receipts (`PAY-`):** Financial acknowledgment of cash/bank transfer receipt. Not an invoice.
- **Credit Notes (`CRN-`):** Immutable correction records linked to original invoice references (Law 4).

### B. Physical Paper Serial Number Capture (`paper_tax_invoice_no`)
- When staff issue a physical paper invoice from the book, the physical serial number (e.g. `INV-BOOK-2083-0042`) is entered into the system.
- The database enforces uniqueness on `(organization_id, paper_tax_invoice_no)` across active invoices, preventing accidental duplicate entries or reused paper numbers.
- Full audit logs capture the staff member ID, timestamp, and linked booking ID upon entry.

### C. Monthly Accountant Pack & VAT Register (T3-23)
- The Sales VAT Register (`sales_vat_register_<month>.csv`) captures:
  1. Transaction Date (Dual AD and BS)
  2. Paper Tax Invoice Number
  3. Buyer Name & Buyer PAN
  4. Taxable Amount (NPR)
  5. 13% Value Added Tax (NPR)
  6. Non-VAT / Exempt Sales (segregated per T3-04)
- Formatted in accordance with standard IRD Sales Book reporting requirements, as reviewed and signed off by the external chartered accountant on 21 November 2026 (`docs/verification/ACCOUNTANT_ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-21.md`).

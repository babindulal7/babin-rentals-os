# Rulings — 24 November 2026

**Scope:** Owner decisions ruled in chat on 24 November 2026 for the December 31 launch plan, operational cutover, rental protection SOP, delivery fee grid, and VAT accounting.

## R-24.1 — Cutover Transition: 1-Month Parallel Dual-Entry Run

- **Owner's words:** "1 month"
- **Reading:** Counter staff will run a 1-month parallel dual-entry period alongside Booqable starting in December. All new bookings are entered in parallel to verify zero discrepancy between systems before Booqable is frozen to read-only ahead of its 18 February 2027 renewal.
- **Changes:** Confirms operational cutover strategy for T9-03 and T9-04. Removes single-day hard cutover risk.

---

## R-24.2 — Official Paper VAT Invoices Issued at Final Return Settlement (O-17)

- **Owner's words:** "Issue official paper VAT bill at Final Return Settlement once all extensions, damages, and final charges are known"
- **Reading:** Official pre-printed IRD paper VAT invoices are written and registered into Babin Rentals OS at the Return & Settlement stage when all final charges (extensions, late hours, damage findings, cleaning) are complete and settled. During an active rental, the OS generates non-tax supporting confirmation/quote PDFs.
- **Changes:** Resolves O-17 in `docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`. Eliminates the need for complex paper credit/debit notes for routine rental extensions or post-return damage additions.

## R-24.3 — Overdue Gear & Payment Recovery Escalation Timeline (PR #402)

- **Owner's words:** "3-Stage Graded Escalation: Day 1 friendly check-in, Day 2–3 formal written notice, Day 7+ legal demand & police filing"
- **Reading:**
  - **Stage 1 (0–24 Hours):** Friendly WhatsApp and phone check-in by Coordinator to confirm shoot extension versus transit delay.
  - **Stage 2 (24–72 Hours):** Formal written notice with item replacement value warning sent by Manager.
  - **Stage 3 (Day 7+):** Formal legal demand letter and police loss report filing executed by Owner.
- **Changes:** Formally codifies Section 5.7 of `docs/WORKFLOWS.md` (Rental Protection, Custody and Payment Recovery SOP).

## R-24.4 — Security Deposit Policy: Per-Product Default, Waivable for VIPs (O-13)

- **Owner's words:** "Per-product default deposit (e.g. Rs 10,000 for FX6/Komodo), waivable for verified repeat clients/VIPs with audited reason"
- **Reading:** High-value gear specifies a default deposit amount in the catalogue. Waiving a deposit requires verified repeat client or VIP status and must be recorded by Manager/Owner with an auditable reason. Deposits remain balance sheet liabilities held in trust (Law 5) and never reduce rental revenue or outstanding balances.
- **Changes:** Resolves O-13 in `docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`.

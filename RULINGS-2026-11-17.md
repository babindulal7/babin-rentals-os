# Rulings — 17 November 2026

**Source:** Babin, answering Claude's questions in chat on 17 Nov 2026. These are Babin's explicit answers.
**Nothing is implemented yet, and nothing is applied to any database (N-5).**

## R-17.1 — TDS deducted by clients from rental invoices

| # | Question | Babin's answer | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-17.1a | Who deducts the TDS? | **Clients, from payments they make to us** | A payment we receive can be net of TDS. |
| R-17.1b | Rate | **1.5% on all rental invoices** | Applies to rental invoices only. |
| R-17.1c | Base | **Before VAT** (taxable value, excluding VAT) | TDS = 1.5% × taxable rental value. |
| R-17.1d | Scope | **Rental invoices only** | Service and other invoices carry no TDS. |
| R-17.1e | Settlement | **Settled at gross.** The invoice counts as fully paid. The cash received is the net amount. The deducted TDS is recorded as a credit we can claim. | The outstanding balance falls by the gross amount, not the cash received. |

**Not decided by this ruling (still open):**
- **Crew-payout TDS rate (O-5).** Still open. R-17.1 covers client payments to us, not payouts to crew.
- **TDS treatment for payments we make to suppliers.** Not covered. The note Babin pasted listed other rates (10% equipment rental, 15% general services), but Babin ruled 1.5% for rental invoices, so those rates are not used.
- **Whether the app should record the TDS credit on the invoice or in a separate ledger.** Not decided. Needs the accountant's view (see O-6 and the accounting pack).

## Implementation notes (for the build card, not yet done)
- Payments are immutable (Law 5). A TDS deduction is recorded as its own row, never as an edit to the original payment.
- Rate and base are fixed by R-17.1b and R-17.1c. They are not free-typed per invoice.
- Revert: remove the TDS line from the rental invoice settlement path; no data is lost, because the deduction rows are additive.

## R-17.3 — Where the client TDS credit is recorded

Babin's answer: **separate TDS ledger**. The credit is not stored on the invoice. It is a separate ledger, linked to the invoice, and the invoice settles at gross (R-17.1e).

## R-17.2a — Staff 1% confirmed (wording resolved)

Babin confirmed: the staff **1%** is a **separate income-tax line**, not TDS deducted from pay. Freelancers stay at **15% TDS**.

## R-17.4 — TDS we deduct from supplier payments

Babin answered: **yes, deduct TDS on supplier payments**. Rates and base:

| Supplier payment | TDS rate |
|---|---|
| Services from VAT-registered suppliers | **1.5%** |
| Services from non-VAT suppliers | **15%** |
| Commission | **15%** |
| Equipment or vehicle rental | **1.5%** |

Base: **before VAT** (taxable amount, excluding VAT).

The deducted TDS is held in the separate TDS ledger (R-17.3), as a liability to deposit with the IRD.
Supplier-side TDS is now ruled. The payment type (for example, service versus commission) decides the rate, so the app needs the supplier payment classified; this is a build detail, not a new rate.

## Mirrored right-side information slider

Babin explicitly requested a slider like the left filter bar for quick information, notes and other right-side content. This supersedes the 7 October always-visible rule for supplemental right rails, including the reflow behavior restated on 18 October and in UI_UX_RULES §11.

- Existing `Rail` / `ReflowRail` content uses one shared `InformationDrawer`, with a labelled 44px toggle in a dedicated right header slot. The drawer starts closed, slides from the right on desktop/tablet, and uses the existing filter sheet on phone. Desktop/tablet reserve its width on the right of the workspace, independently of left filters.
- Content stays mounted while hidden so note drafts survive closing. Existing role checks, data reads and writes remain at their original sites. Team Internal Notes uses the same drawer presentation; only one drawer owns each edge at a time on desktop, and narrow screens open one side drawer at a time to preserve working space.
- Supplemental summaries and previews use the drawer at every width. Essential floor checklist/completion columns remain in their existing operation workspaces; they contain required workflow controls rather than supplemental information.
- Calendar Full View keeps its selected-event workspace visible because that overlay covers the global header toggle.
- No permission, money rule, capability, database migration or business calculation changes.

Changed: `InformationDrawer.tsx`, `FilterDrawer.tsx`, `Page.tsx`, workspace header slot/context, right-rail caller branches, `InternalNotesDrawer.tsx`, `ui-v2.css`.

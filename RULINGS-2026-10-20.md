Babin explicitly requested a slider like the left filter bar for quick information, notes and other right-side content. This supersedes the 7 October always-visible rule for supplemental right rails, including the reflow behavior restated on 18 October and in UI_UX_RULES §11.

Desktop/tablet reserve its width on the right of the workspace, independently of left filters.
- Content stays mounted while hidden so note drafts survive closing. Existing role checks, data reads and writes remain at their original sites. - Calendar Full View keeps its selected-event workspace visible because that overlay covers the global header toggle.

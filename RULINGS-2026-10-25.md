- Migration 0175 enforces the existing verified usable Return requirement for
  partial serial and quantity receipts. Reservation adjustments commit at the
  persisted Return session seal; open or failed sessions retain their holds.
  Branch grants apply to direct writes and quantity reads. Completed seals are
  immutable; new facts require a new open pass. Verified quantity receipts are
  private, bounded, and corrected by append-only compensating movements.
- A persisted usable receipt cannot be reclassified as damaged during an
  interrupted retry. The app retains the original attempt identity and refuses
  conflicting retry facts. Unknown persisted quantity history blocks entry.
- Routine cleaning alone does not block usable returned stock, as already ruled
  on 24 October. Damage, loss, maintenance, retirement, unresolved findings, and
  actual QC defects still block it; a Reset timestamp does not erase a defect.
- Section saves preserve unsaved sibling drafts and only advance the saved
  section's baseline. Retry identity survives ambiguous responses. Unsupported
  image/relationship persistence, unavailable evidence capture, and unavailable
  quantity-only Reset remain explicitly unavailable rather than reporting success.
- Existing stricter role sets win: stock Add/Retire remains Owner/Admin/Manager.
  No money-blind role gains financial fields or payloads. Money remains integer
  paisa, with rupee conversion only at documented display/legacy DB boundaries.

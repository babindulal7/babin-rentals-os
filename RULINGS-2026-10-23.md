**Waiting on Babin — not done, because removing them would break something already ruled:**

- **KPI tile (i).** It carries the tile's definition (Law L1, "every money tile carries a one-line definition on the tile"), its zero explanation (L2) and a `note`, fed by about 260 `note` / `definition` / `zeroExplanation` fields across 44 module files. Removing the icon alone leaves all of that unreachable; showing it on the tile face adds text. `KpiStrip.info.test.tsx` pins the current design.
- **Quick-information drawer button** (RULINGS-2026-10-20). The drawer holds real page content on about 20 screens (rental summary on New Rental, deposits on Payments, client / bundle / warehouse summaries, event detail). With the button gone there is no way to open it. The choice is where that content goes: inline on the page, or kept behind a different control.

**Docs not updated:** the dated screen catalogues under `docs/pages/*` and `docs/reference/SCREENS.md` still list `FooterNote` as it was on their dates. They are historical.

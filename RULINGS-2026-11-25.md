## R-25.3 — Fail-Closed OS Physical Precedence & GiST Reservation Rules

- **Owner's ruling:** Fail-closed OS physical precedence — Babin Rentals OS GiST reservation rules prevent double-booking. If a conflict occurs, staff must resolve the schedule/serial conflict before gear leaves, log the event in the cutover incident sheet, and alert the Coordinator.
- **Reading:** The strict mathematical GiST exclusion engine of Babin Rentals OS governs physical warehouse availability. If Booqable allows an overlapping or conflicting order, Babin Rentals OS refuses checkout until staff adjust the reservation or assign an alternative serial unit. The incident is logged in the cutover incident register.
- **Changes:** Enforces physical custody safety and prevents double-booking disputes during cutover.

## R-25.4 — Automatic "On Stop" at Recovery Stage 2 (24 Hours Overdue)

- **Owner's ruling:** Automatic "On Stop" at Stage 2 (24 hours overdue without approved extension) — The OS places the client account On Stop, blocking new bookings unless overridden with an audited Manager/Owner reason.
- **Reading:** When a rental passes 24 hours past scheduled return time without an authorized extension logged in the OS, the client account is automatically flagged "On Stop". Counter staff are blocked from generating new quotes or checking out equipment for this client without an explicit, audited override by Manager or Owner.

# Rulings — 16 October 2026

## Booking Detail phone header: a one-word stage action, Search in the More

Follow-up 1 from the 15 Oct header triage (`RULINGS-2026-10-15.md` rows 11–18). At 375px,
booking 873 (On Rent, "Receive Overdue Return", 182px) overflowed the header by **37px**. The
title got **0px**, the avatar sat off-screen (x 368–412) and Operations overlapped the back
button. The phone header holds back 44, the title, Operations 44, More 44, the stage action,
Search 44 and the avatar 44. With the gaps, those fixed controls leave the title **83px minus the
stage action**, so no single change could fix it. An icon-only stage action alone left 39px.
Build-time authority: CLAUDE.md §15. Phone only (< 768px). Tablet and desktop are unchanged.
Each row is flippable by number.

# Rulings — 22 October 2026

## "Activate membership" — the tier change is real

Babin: "activate membership". Asked what that meant, he chose **make the tier change real** (not renewal of a lapsed membership), and **leave the fee blank**. The Change Tier dialog's save had been deliberately blocked ("no tier-change action exists"), because a tier lives in `client_memberships` and nothing wrote it. No existing permission-matrix cell was widened; one capability is added, mirroring a policy that already exists.
